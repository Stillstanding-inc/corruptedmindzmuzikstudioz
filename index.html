The man

Mark Workman is a great rapper out of Grand Bay, Alabama — a hooligan from back home, and James Bosarge's brother. It runs the same both ways: Mark considers James his brother, and James considers Mark his. Mark is one of James's two home boys of all time.

At the table

Mark sat around the same table the rest of these stories sat around. James's mother was writing her book, The Stories This Table Can Tell, and James is completing it — Mark Workman is one of the stories this table can tell. His own label, Corrupted Mindz Music, carries his great works. Corrupted Mindz Music is his — his own label, standing on its own — and it stands here under the Still Standing front door.
